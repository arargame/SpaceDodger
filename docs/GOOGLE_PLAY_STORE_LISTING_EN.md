## 2. Updated Full Description (Incorporating World Ranking / Leaderboard Challenge)

```text
Blast off into a thrilling retro space adventure! 

Space Dodger is a fast-paced classic arcade shoot 'em up that tests your reflexes and dodging skills. Take command of your starship, navigate through dense asteroid fields, and battle endless waves of hostile alien fleets across deep space.

Whether you're looking for quick casual action or aiming to master all 100 challenging levels, Space Dodger delivers pure nostalgic arcade excitement with modern, ultra-responsive touch controls.

🚀 KEY FEATURES:

⭐ 100 ACTION-PACKED LEVELS
Embark on an epic cosmic campaign. Each stage brings new enemy formations, dynamic hazards, and intense bullet patterns.

⭐ EPIC BOSS ENCOUNTERS
Face off against powerful alien motherships and bosses with unique attack strategies and movement patterns.

⭐ GLOBAL LEADERBOARDS & WORLD RANKING
Think you're the sharpest pilot in deep space? Compete with players worldwide! Tap the "WORLD RANKING" button on the high score screen to access Google Play Games leaderboards, track your rank against top aces across the globe, and fight for the top spot on the universal leaderboard!

⭐ POWERFUL UPGRADES & SHIELD SYSTEMS
Collect in-game power-ups to upgrade your laser firepower, activate impenetrable energy shields, and trigger rapid-fire boosts.

⭐ RETRO PIXEL ART & NOSTALGIC SOUNDTRACK
Experience classic 90s handheld and arcade gaming vibes with handcrafted pixel graphics and dynamic chiptune sound effects.

⭐ 100% OFFLINE PLAY
No Wi-Fi? No internet? No problem! Play anywhere, anytime without an active internet connection.

⭐ INTUITIVE TOUCH CONTROLS
Silky-smooth, responsive controls designed specifically for mobile touchscreens. Easy to learn, challenging to master!

⭐ HIGH SCORES & STATS
Track your progress, beat your personal best scores, and prove yourself as the ultimate space pilot.

HOW TO PLAY:
- Drag or touch to steer your starship.
- Dodge incoming asteroids, lasers, and enemy ships.
- Collect weapon upgrades and shields to dominate the battlefield.
- Defeat the boss at the end of each sector to unlock new challenges!
- Tap "WORLD RANKING" to challenge top pilots and compare your best scores on the global leaderboard!

Gear up, pilot! The galaxy needs a hero. Download Space Dodger now and start dodging!
```
